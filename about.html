Our story

Marketing got split into specialisms. Brands got the seams.

A brand agency hands over a deck. A media agency runs the ads. A production company shoots the film. A web studio builds the site. Each does good work, and the customer sees four different brands. Ludic Labs exists to close those seams: one team that can set the direction, make the things, put them in front of people, and learn from what happens.

We’re a full-service digital marketing and creative agency, which is a long way of saying we take responsibility for the whole thing working.

01

Curiosity

We start every engagement by asking more questions than feels polite. The business, the customer, the category, the thing nobody has tried. Good ideas are usually hiding in an answer someone considered obvious.

02

Craft

Strategy, design, writing, photography, code, and media buying are all crafts. We hold them to the same standard: clear, considered, and made to be used, not just approved.

03

Disciplined experimentation

Every launch is a test with a question attached. We decide in advance what we’re trying to learn, measure it honestly, and change course when the evidence says so. We don’t promise outcomes we can’t control. We promise to find out.
