Brand Identity — Ludic Labs
Identity system · internal reference

The Playground of Precision.

Two L forms, rotated 180° to each other, lock together to make one square. They never quite touch: the gap between them holds a single acid-yellow dot, the “ball” that turns a structure into a game. One L carries a notched corner, the deliberate flaw that says this was drawn by hand, not stamped. Every graphic on the site is built from these three parts: the L, the gap, the dot.
